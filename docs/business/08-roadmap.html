Phased roadmap

Tied to funding tranches and to the two questions that decide the business: what does an utterance cost, and does it work in a loud shop in someone else's voice.

The sequencing rule. Every phase must close a gap in the evidence register before it adds a feature. A prototype that grows features without closing gaps becomes harder to fund, not easier.

What we will not do

  • Manufacture hardware. Different economics, different capital, different investor.
  • Hold payment funds. Licensing and float risk we have no reason to take.
  • Chase F&B depth. Petpooja has a decade of it. We win at the counter that still uses paper.
  • Sell shop data before the shop benefits. A brand paying for shelf intelligence is not paying to help the shopkeeper negotiate. Opt-in, late, or not at all.
